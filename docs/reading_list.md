# References & Reading List

This project is inspired by and conceptually grounded in academic and
practitioner literature on quantitative trading, backtesting, and risk management.

---

## Backtesting, Walk-Forward & Overfitting

- Bailey, D., Borwein, J., López de Prado, M., & Zhu, Q.  
  **The Probability of Backtest Overfitting**  
  Introduces the concept of PBO and explains why many trading strategies fail out-of-sample.

- Bailey, D. & López de Prado, M.  
  **The Deflated Sharpe Ratio**  
  Corrects for multiple testing bias in strategy evaluation.

- López de Prado, M.  
  **Advances in Financial Machine Learning**  
  Chapters on backtesting, cross-validation, and financial data leakage.

- Aronson, D.  
  **Evidence-Based Technical Analysis**  
  Practical treatment of testing trading rules with statistical rigor.

---

## Risk Management & Position Sizing

- Kelly, J. L.  
  **A New Interpretation of Information Rate**  
  Original paper introducing the Kelly Criterion.

- MacLean, L., Thorp, E., & Ziemba, W.  
  **The Kelly Capital Growth Investment Criterion**  
  Extensions and practical considerations of Kelly sizing in portfolios.

- Meucci, A.  
  **Risk and Asset Allocation**  
  Modern framework for risk-based portfolio construction.

- Jorion, P.  
  **Value at Risk: The New Benchmark for Managing Financial Risk**  
  Foundational reference for risk measurement and drawdown control.

---

## Volatility & Market Regimes

- Yang, D. & Zhang, Q.  
  **Drift-Independent Volatility Estimation Based on High, Low, Open and Close Prices**  
  Introduces the Yang–Zhang volatility estimator used in this project.

- Engle, R.  
  **Autoregressive Conditional Heteroskedasticity (ARCH)**  
  Foundational work on volatility clustering.

---

## Machine Learning in Trading

- Gu, S., Kelly, B., & Xiu, D.  
  **Empirical Asset Pricing via Machine Learning**  
  Demonstrates the use of ML models in cross-sectional return prediction.

- Fischer, T. & Krauss, C.  
  **Deep Learning with Long Short-Term Memory Networks for Financial Market Predictions**  
  Early application of deep learning to financial time series.

- Dixon, M., Halperin, I., & Bilokon, P.  
  **Machine Learning in Finance**  
  End-to-end view of ML-based trading systems.

---

## Trading System Design & Practice

- Chan, E.  
  **Algorithmic Trading: Winning Strategies and Their Rationale**  
  Practical guide to building realistic trading strategies.

- Pardo, R.  
  **The Evaluation and Optimization of Trading Strategies**  
  Walk-forward testing and robustness analysis.

---

## Notes

This reading list reflects the conceptual foundation of the project.
The implementation focuses on practicality and robustness rather than
direct replication of any single paper.
